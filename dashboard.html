⚙️ One-time setup needed

This dashboard reads student scores from your Google Sheet by fetching its published CSV link. That link hasn't been added yet.

  1. Open your Google Sheet (the one your reading portal already sends scores to).
  2. Go to File → Share → Publish to web.
  3. Under "Link", choose the specific sheet/tab your scores are in (not "Entire document"). Under the format dropdown, choose Comma-separated values (.csv).
  4. Click Publish, confirm, and copy the URL it gives you.
  5. Open this file (dashboard.html) in a text editor, find const SHEET_CSV_URL near the top of the script, and paste the URL between the quotes.
  6. Save the file and re-upload it to GitHub.

Privacy note: "Publish to web" makes that sheet viewable by anyone with the link (not searchable, but not password-protected either). Since this sheet contains student names and scores, only share this dashboard's link with staff who should see it, and avoid publishing sheets that contain more than what's needed here.
